Explainable AI Architecture

How The Analysis Engine Works

Unlike opaque black-box checkers, our multi-stage pipeline delivers fully transparent, reproducible, and explainable evaluations.

1. Document Parsing

Extracts layout-independent text via PyMuPDF (PDF) and python-docx. Segmenters detect sections like Education, Experience, Projects, and Skills.

2. NLP Skill Matching

Scans token n-grams against role benchmarks. Normalizes industry aliases (e.g., K8s → Kubernetes, ML → Machine Learning) without false positives.

3. TF-IDF & Scoring

scikit-learn TF-IDF vectorization computes semantic cosine similarity against target profiles. Heuristic scoring transparently grades 6 dimensions out of 100.

4. Smart Feedback

Generates prioritized, evidence-backed recommendations targeting missing skills, weak bullet structures, and formatting gaps. Never fabricates achievements.
